Patient Confidentiality

Privacy Policy & HIPAA Notice

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Houston, Texas

1. Commitment to Patient Privacy

Radiant Med Spa ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting the confidentiality, security, and integrity of your personal and health-related information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information collected through our website, appointment inquiry portals, and in-clinic intake forms.

2. Health Information (HIPAA Compliance)

Any protected health information (PHI) collected during your medical aesthetic consultation, including skin imaging, treatment records, medical histories, and clinical photography, is governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable Texas medical privacy statutes. We do not sell, rent, or disclose your clinical information to third-party advertisers.

3. Information Collected via Our Website

When you use our website, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, requested treatment interest, and schedule preferences. We use this data solely to respond to inquiries, confirm appointment bookings, and provide care updates.

4. Communications & SMS Policy

By submitting an inquiry or appointment reservation, you opt in to receive appointment reminders and coordination messages via phone, SMS, or email. You may opt out of promotional messages at any time by replying STOP or contacting our concierge desk.

5. Security Safeguards

We utilize industry-standard electronic, physical, and administrative safeguards, including TLS encryption and role-based access control, to safeguard your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.
