Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, physician-supervised answers to the 14 most important questions regarding aesthetic treatments, candidate safety, scheduling, and clinic policies.

A medical spa (med spa) is a hybrid facility combining a tranquil, relaxing aesthetic environment with clinical, physician-supervised cosmetic treatments. Unlike a traditional day spa that focuses strictly on relaxation and superficial massage, a med spa operates under licensed medical supervision to provide advanced procedures—such as neuromodulators (Botox), hyaluronic dermal fillers, Sciton BBL lasers, chemical peels, and medical microneedling—that target deeper dermal layers.

You do not need to diagnose yourself or choose a specific procedure beforehand. The best starting point is scheduling a Comprehensive Aesthetic Consultation. Our licensed clinicians evaluate your skin barrier, listen to your aesthetic goals, and design a phased roadmap matching your comfort level, budget, and social calendar.

Yes. Under Texas medical aesthetics regulations and our clinic safety standards, every new patient undergoes a medical intake and skin evaluation prior to treatment. This ensures that procedures are medically safe, contraindications are screened, and realistic expectations are aligned. In many cases, consultation and same-day treatment can be scheduled together if clinically suitable.

You can request an appointment through our online Appointment Concierge, call our front desk directly at (713) 555-0198, or send a request via our Contact page. Please note that submitting an online form is an inquiry request; our patient concierge coordinator will promptly contact you to confirm schedule availability and review pre-treatment instructions.

Most cosmetic aesthetic treatments are not permanent because skin and facial tissues continue their natural biological aging process. Neuromodulators typically last 3 to 4 months; hyaluronic dermal fillers last 9 to 18 months; and laser/photofacial outcomes persist for years when supported by daily sunscreen and periodic maintenance. Permanent claims cannot be ethically or clinically made for non-surgical cosmetic services.

Before any clinical facial: (1) Discontinue prescription topical retinoids, exfoliating AHAs/BHAs, and harsh physical scrubs for 48 to 72 hours; (2) Avoid intense direct sun exposure or sunburn; (3) Refrain from waxing or threading facial hair for 5 days; and (4) Arrive with clean, bare skin if possible so our clinician can assess your natural barrier condition.

Downtime varies depending on the specific procedure. HydraFacials, dermaplaning, and non-ablative laser facials have zero social downtime (you can immediately return to work). Neuromodulators have mild pinpoint pinkness for 20 minutes. Chemical peels and SkinPen microneedling involve 24 to 72 hours of redness or light flaking. Deeper fractional lasers involve 3 to 5 days of manageable peeling. We always review recovery timelines before starting.

Pricing reflects the individual treatment plan, product dosage, and anatomical complexity. Clinical facials start from $165 to $245; neuromodulator dosing is tailored at $13–$15 per unit; dermal fillers range from $650+ per syringe; and advanced laser photofacials start from $350. We provide full, transparent cost breakdowns during your consultation with zero hidden fees.

However, certain aggressive modalities (e.g., medium-depth chemical peels followed immediately by microneedling) must be spaced 2 to 4 weeks apart to prevent barrier trauma. We understand that schedules shift. We request at least 24 hours advance notice for any rescheduling or cancellations. This allows us to offer the reserved treatment suite to other guests on our waitlist. You can reschedule by phone at (713) 555-0198 or through your confirmation link.

General preparation includes: (1) Stay well hydrated; (2) Avoid blood-thinning supplements (fish oil, vitamin E, ibuprofen, aspirin) and alcohol for 48–72 hours prior to injectable appointments to reduce bruising; (3) Avoid tanning or sunburn for at least 4 weeks prior to any laser therapy; and (4) Notify us if you have any history of oral cold sores, active infections, or recent vaccinations.

All clinical aesthetic procedures carry inherent considerations. Common minor side effects include temporary redness, pinpoint bruising, mild swelling, and localized sensitivity that resolve over several days. Less common risks include infection, pigment changes (hyper- or hypopigmentation), or allergic reactions. All treatments at Radiant are performed under physician supervision with hospital-level sterility to minimize risk.

Suitability depends on your Fitzpatrick Skin Prototype (I through VI). Non-heat treatments such as HydraFacials, SkinPen microneedling, and GentleMax Pro 1064nm Nd:YAG lasers are safe and clinically validated across all skin prototypes, including melanin-rich skin. High-heat intense pulsed light (IPL) requires careful calibration to prevent post-inflammatory pigment alterations. We determine your skin type during consultation.
